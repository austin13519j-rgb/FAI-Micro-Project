1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT IN SIMPLE WORDS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In hospitals, when one surgery ends, the Operation Theatre (OT) cannot be used 
immediately for the next patient. Several things must happen first:
1. The room must be cleaned and disinfected.
2. The used surgical tools must be sterilised and cooled down.
3. Fresh supplies, medicines, and disposables must be restocked.
4. The surgical instrument table must be prepared by a specialized nurse.
5. The next patient must be checked and rolled into the theatre.

This entire gap between two surgeries is called the "Turnaround Time".

If nurses and hospital staff do this scheduling by hand or guess, surgeries get 
delayed, staff get double-booked, or patients wait needlessly.

This project creates a smart "Forward Planning Agent" (an AI planning system) 
that takes the surgery end time, available staff, and cleaning protocol, and 
automatically calculates the exact minute-by-minute schedule, detects parallel 
tasks, checks staff availability, and warns if the next surgery will be on time 
or delayed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE FORWARD PLANNING AGENT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Forward planning means we start at the beginning time (when previous surgery ends) 
and move forward in time step-by-step, checking rules at every minute:

Rule 1 - Dependency Rule: 
  A task cannot begin until all prerequisite tasks are 100% finished.
  (Example: You cannot set up sterile instruments before they have finished cooling.)

Rule 2 - Staff Availability Rule:
  A task cannot begin unless the required staff member is on duty.
  (If the cleaning staff is marked absent, the plan is flagged as INFEASIBLE.)

Rule 3 - No Double-Booking Rule:
  One staff member cannot do two different jobs at the same time.
  (If Support Staff is restocking, they cannot transfer the patient simultaneously.)

Rule 4 - Parallel Execution Rule:
  If two tasks have their prerequisites met and use different staff members, 
  they can run at the same time to save valuable minutes!
  (Example: Cleaning the room and sterilising instruments can happen in parallel.)

Rule 5 - Patient Readiness Rule:
  The patient cannot be moved into the OT until both the patient is ready in 
  pre-op holding AND the OT room setup is completely finished.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE 9 WORKFLOW TASKS IN EVERY PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Task ID | Task Name                  | Duration    | Assigned Staff     | Prerequisite
--------|----------------------------|-------------|--------------------|--------------
T1      | Previous Surgery End       | 0 min       | N/A                | None (Start)
T2      | Clean Operation Theatre    | 20-50 min   | Cleaning Staff     | T1
T3      | Sterilise Instruments      | 30 min      | Sterilisation Staff| T1
T4      | Mandatory Cooling/Waiting  | 10 min      | Passive (No staff) | T3
T5      | Restock OT Supplies        | 15 min      | Support Staff      | T2
T6      | Set Up Surgical Instruments| 15 min      | OT Nurse           | T4 and T5
T7      | Check Patient Readiness    | 0 min       | Pre-op Holding     | None
T8      | Transfer Patient to OT     | 10 min      | Support + Nurse    | T6 and T7
T9      | Confirm OT Ready & Handover| 0 min       | OT Nurse           | T8 (Complete)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW THE SYSTEM DECIDES THE FINAL STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The algorithm calculates:
- Calculated Start Time = The exact time Task T8 finishes and T9 completes.
- Delay = Maximum(0, Calculated Start Time - Scheduled Start Time).

Status Output:
1. "ON TIME"   -> If Calculated Start Time <= Next Surgery Scheduled Time.
2. "DELAYED"   -> If Calculated Start Time > Next Surgery Scheduled Time (shows delay in minutes).
3. "INFEASIBLE"-> If a required staff member is absent and no qualified substitute exists.

6. THE 6 PAGES ON THE WEB DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dashboard (/)
   - Shows live statistics: Total plans, On-time surgeries, Delayed count, Average turnaround.
   - Current OT readiness status card and upcoming surgery alert.
   - Recent plans summary table with 1-click links.

2. Create Turnaround Plan (/create)
   - Clean form divided into 5 sections:
     A. Surgery Times (previous end, next scheduled, patient ready).
     B. Cleaning Protocol (Normal 20 min vs Deep cleaning 50 min).
     C. Sterilisation & Cooling duration.
     D. Restocking, Setup & Patient Transfer duration.
     E. Staff Availability toggles (Cleaning, Sterilisation, OT Nurse, Support Staff).
   - "Generate Turnaround Plan" button calls the Python forward planner.

3. Generated Turnaround Plan & Gantt (/plan/[id])
   - Visual interactive Gantt chart showing the full timeline.
   - Parallel tasks indicator (shows which tasks overlap safely).
   - Staff conflicts and assignments breakdown.
   - Big status badge (ON TIME / DELAYED / INFEASIBLE).

4. Scenario Comparison (/scenarios)
   - Runs 4 standard clinical cases simultaneously:
     Case 1: Normal cleaning + All staff available (Best Case)
     Case 2: Deep cleaning + All staff available (Decontamination Case)
     Case 3: Normal cleaning + Staff member missing (Staff Shortage Case)
     Case 4: Deep cleaning + Staff member missing (Worst Case)
   - Recharts bar chart comparing turnaround durations side-by-side.

5. Medical Handover Report (/reports/[id])
   - Formal hospital handover audit summary.
   - "Download CSV Report" button for hospital record-keeping.

6. Plan History (/history)
   - Searchable and filterable archive of all past surgery plans.
   - Filter by status (All, On Time, Delayed, Infeasible).

10. COMMON VIVA / EVALUATION QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why is this called "Forward Planning"?
A1: Because the algorithm starts from an initial known time (when the previous 
    surgery completes) and applies forward state progression, adding actions 
    whose preconditions are satisfied until the goal state (OT ready & patient 
    transferred) is reached.

Q2: Why not use Machine Learning or Deep Learning for this?
A2: Medical and surgical scheduling requires 100% deterministic safety guarantees. 
    A neural network might hallucinate or violate a mandatory cooling waiting time. 
    Rule-based deterministic forward planning guarantees that zero safety rules 
    and zero staffing conflicts are ever violated.

Q3: How does the system handle parallel tasks?
A3: When a task finishes, the scheduler checks which uncompleted tasks have all 
    their prerequisites completed. If two such tasks require different staff 
    members (like Cleaning using Cleaning Staff and Sterilisation using Sterilisation 
    Staff), both are scheduled to start at the exact same minute.

Q4: What happens if a staff member is absent?
A4: The staff manager verifies required qualifications. If an essential role 
    (like OT Nurse) is marked unavailable and cannot be staffed, the planner 
    immediately halts with an explicit "INFEASIBLE - RESOURCE CONSTRAINT" status 
    instead of producing a dangerous or false schedule.
